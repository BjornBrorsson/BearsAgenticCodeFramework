# Skill: Session Handoff & Multi-Agent Resume (Startup Edition)

> **Load When:** Pausing an unfinished feature, hitting context limits, or switching between agents/sessions.

---

## Handoff Checklist
1. Update `./tasks/<feature>/PROJECT_LOG.md` and `PROJECT_MEMORIES.md` with what is done and any quirks found.
2. Update the active card in `Tickets/Ongoing/` (check off completed `- [x]` boxes and append a dated `## Work Log` note).
3. Write `./tasks/<feature>/HANDOFF_STATE.md` listing: (1) Exact file/function (`~Line`) in progress, (2) What remains, and (3) Any open blocker.
