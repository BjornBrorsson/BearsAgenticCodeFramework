# Skill: Requirements & Spec Writing (Startup Edition)

> **Load When:** Turning rough ideas, notes, or issues into actionable specs or **Agentic Kanban** cards (`Tickets/Backlog/`).

---

## Protocol
1. **Separate Facts from Assumptions:** Write down what is known and ask 2–4 sharp questions on any ambiguous business/data rules.
2. **Write Concrete Checkboxes (`- [ ]`):** Each acceptance criterion must be binary and testable (including empty-input and error behavior).
3. **Use Kanban for >3 Tasks:** Place ready cards in `Tickets/Backlog/Ready/` and rough ideas in `Tickets/Backlog/Needs further specification/` (see `kanban_workflow.md`).
